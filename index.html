Common Questions

Got Questions?
We've Got Answers.

How long does a website project take? +
Project timelines depend on scope. A standard 5-10 page website typically takes 7-14 days. A custom web application or e-commerce store ranges from 3-8 weeks. We always provide a detailed timeline in our proposal before work begins — no surprises.
Do you work with international clients? +
Absolutely. We work with clients across 15+ countries. Communication happens via WhatsApp, Slack, Zoom, or email — whichever works best for you. Time zone differences have never been an issue for our team.
What's included in your SEO service? +
Our SEO service includes full technical audit and fixes, on-page optimization for all target pages, keyword research, content strategy, internal linking, schema markup, and monthly link-building outreach. You get detailed monthly reports showing ranking progress and traffic growth.
How do payments work? +
We work on a milestone-based payment structure. Typically 50% to start, and 50% on completion. For larger projects, we split into 3 milestone payments. We accept PayPal, bank transfer, Wise, and direct payment. A full contract is signed before any work begins.
Do you provide ongoing support after launch? +
Yes. All packages include a post-launch support period (7 days for Starter, 3 months for Scale, 6 months for Accelerate). After that, we offer affordable monthly maintenance retainers covering updates, security, backups, and performance monitoring.
Will I own the code and design files? +
Yes — 100%. Upon final payment, full ownership of all source code, design files (Figma), and assets transfers to you. We hand over complete credentials for all platforms, hosting, and domains. No lock-in, ever.
Can you help with an existing website that needs improvement? +
Absolutely. We handle redesigns, performance optimization, CRO audits, SEO overhauls, and feature additions for existing sites. We'll do a free audit first to identify the highest-impact improvements for your specific situation.
How do I get started? +
Simply fill out the contact form below or click "Get Free Consultation" in the navigation. We'll schedule a 30-minute discovery call within 24 hours to understand your goals, answer questions, and provide a detailed proposal — completely free, no obligation.
